# 🏛️ **PROJECT TWO — LARGE-SCALE RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT**

### **AMIRIAN CORPORATION 2026**

## **In the Name of God**

**Project Two** is the second major construction initiative of **AMIRIAN CORPORATION 2026** within the game environment, focusing exclusively on the planning, design, construction, and expansion of large-scale residential developments. 🏘️🏗️

The project has been established with the objective of creating **modern residential districts, large housing complexes, luxury homes, multi-story buildings, and extensive residential communities** throughout the game world.

## 🎯 **Project Objectives**

The primary objectives of Project Two include:

* Development of large residential districts
* Construction of modern and luxury homes
* Development of multi-story residential buildings
* Creation of large housing complexes
* Expansion of residential areas within the game
* Development of organized streets and residential infrastructure
* Construction of custom residential properties based on specific designs
* Establishment of distinctive and high-quality living environments

## 🏠 **Residential Development**

Project Two is designed to support different types of residential construction, including:

**Modern Houses**
Contemporary homes designed with clean architecture and efficient layouts.

**Luxury Houses**
Large and high-level residential properties with additional facilities and detailed designs.

**Large Residential Buildings**
Multi-story buildings intended to accommodate multiple residential units.

**Large Residential Complexes**
Extensive developments combining multiple buildings, residential areas, internal roads, and supporting infrastructure.

**Custom Houses**
Residential projects designed according to specific requirements and individual concepts within the game.
